# Exact-cost risk-cascade v2 evidence

This directory contains the summary evidence used by the current research report.

## Development result

The frozen repeated-grouped development analysis evaluated three signed-value model families across the required selective comparators.

No signed-value family produced a jointly feasible development budget. Each family passed only 1 of 16 primary pairwise cost-equivalence comparisons. Accordingly, no router family was selected and no router-superiority claim is made.

## Interpretation boundary

The development budget anchors use measured development component timings for preselection. They are not final deployment cost certificates.

Formal joint FPR-and-cost certification requires independent fresh calibration data. Confirmatory superiority additionally requires fresh source- and time-separated data with the prespecified stronger multi-rater labels. Those data were not available and those claim gates were not executed.

The protected legacy `final_test` and `held_out_shift` splits remained sealed during v2.
